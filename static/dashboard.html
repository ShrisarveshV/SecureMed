Algorithm Primary Deployment Domain Strengths Trade-offs / Limitations

Architectural Concepts & FIPS Standards
Key Encapsulation (KEM)

Unlike RSA or Diffie-Hellman, ML-KEM outputs a ciphertext that encapsulates a random shared secret. The recipient decapsulates it using their private key. It cannot encrypt arbitrary files directly.

Hybrid Cryptography

Combines classical X25519 and ML-KEM-768. An attacker must break both the classical elliptic-curve problem and the module-lattice problem to compromise the derived session key.

Digital Signatures (DSA)

ML-DSA provides non-repudiation and firmware integrity. Because post-quantum signatures are larger (2.4 KB to 4.6 KB), they are evaluated separately from telemetry packet MAC tags.

SecureMed / Measurement Hardware

Measurement Hardware & Energy Instrumentation

Hardware sensor diagnostics, timer resolution verification, and operating-system power telemetry inspection.

Energy Measurement Instrumentation Status Unavailable
Non-Negotiable Rule: Never estimate energy from execution time or CPU TDP. Because this laptop currently lacks a calibrated external USB-C power analyzer or hardware RAPL MSR sensor driver, energy consumption is marked Unavailable.

Why Energy Cannot Be Inferred from Execution Time:

1. Modern CPUs dynamically scale clock frequencies (e.g. 800 MHz idle to 4.9 GHz boost). Execution duration does not correlate linearly with milliwatts consumed.
2. Instruction mix varies: AES-NI uses dedicated hardware circuits drawing different instantaneous power compared to bit-sliced pure software logic.
3. In IoT and medical devices, radio transmission energy often dominates processor core energy by an order of magnitude.

Supported External Hardware Analyzers:

  • Nordic Semiconductor Power Profiler Kit II (PPK2)

  • Keysight N6705C DC Power Analyzer

Mathematical Formulas & Metric Definitions

Throughput (Megabytes per second):

Throughput (MB/s) = (Payload_Bytes * Repetitions) / (Total_Wall_Seconds * 1,048,576)

Wire Overhead Percentage:

Overhead (%) = ((Wire_Ciphertext_Bytes + Nonce + Tag - Plaintext_Bytes) / Plaintext_Bytes) * 100

95% Confidence Interval (CI):

CI_95 = Mean +/- 1.96 * (Standard_Deviation / sqrt(N))

Anti-Replay Window (RFC 4303):

Window Range = [Highest_Sequence - 64 + 1, Highest_Sequence]
